Neocore RT · For Physical AI & Robotics Teams

The bottleneck in Physical AI is motion data. Collect it without suits.

Neocore RT feeds real-world motion into imitation learning and teleoperation pipelines, captured from standard RGB cameras — with operators in street clothes behaving like real workers, not suited-up rehearsers.

27-joint
Hand & finger tracking per hand — native to the system, not a bolt-on glove. Finger-level demonstration data for dexterity learning.
<250 ms
End-to-end teleoperation latency — from camera to robot control loop, with zero gear on the operator.
cm-class
Absolute position accuracy — sufficient for most robotic learning tasks. If you need sub-millimetre, optical still wins.
Skeleton-only output
Privacy mode — anonymised skeleton stream only; raw video can be discarded at the edge for compliance use.
